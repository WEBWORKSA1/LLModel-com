Write, research and build with us

LLModel is growing a small remote team of people who know LLMs and explain them well. Roles are paid, flexible and project-based to start. If you've shipped with LLMs or written about them clearly, we'd like to hear from you.

RemotePaid, project-basedApplications reviewed weekly

Open roles

  • LLM research writer

    Write and update in-depth guides and comparisons. You test claims, cite primary sources and explain trade-offs in plain English. Paid per article.

  • Model data analyst

    Keep prices, context windows, licenses and release data current across providers, with a source for every change. A few hours a week.

  • Front-end developer

    Build fast, accessible tools in plain JavaScript: calculators, comparisons and visualizations that run in the browser. Contract.

  • Video producer

    Script, record and edit short explainers on models, prices and tools for our YouTube channel. Paid per video.

  • Community and contest manager

    Run contest seasons end to end: briefs, judging logistics, winner features and reader questions. Part-time.

  • Partnerships lead

    Build relationships with AI vendors and implementation agencies for sponsorships and our partner network. Commission-based.

Frequently asked questions

Are these full-time jobs?

Not yet. Roles start as paid, project-based or part-time contracts and can grow as the site grows. Tell us your availability in the form.

Do I need to be in a particular country?

No. We work remotely and asynchronously. We do need you to be legally able to work as a contractor where you live.

Can I use AI tools in my work?

Yes, as long as you verify every fact against primary sources and follow our editorial policy. We care about accuracy and clear thinking, not about how the first draft was typed.
