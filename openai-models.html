OpenAI models

OpenAI is the San Francisco company behind ChatGPT and the GPT model families. Its current API lineup is the GPT-6 family: GPT-6 Astra for the most demanding reasoning and coding work, GPT-6.1 Sol as a lower-cost option close to Astra, and GPT-6 Luna for high-volume tasks, with several GPT-5.x models still available. In August 2025 it also released gpt-oss-120b and gpt-oss-20b, Apache 2.0 open-weight reasoning models you can run on your own hardware or through most inference providers.

Frequently asked questions

What models does OpenAI offer?

As of October 10, 2026, we track 7 OpenAI models: GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol, gpt-oss-120b, GPT-6 Luna, gpt-oss-20b, GPT-5.5, GPT-5.6 Sol. Status, prices and context windows for each are in the table on this page.

How do I access OpenAI models?

Through OpenAI's developer platform (developers.openai.com/api/docs), in the ChatGPT app, and for many models through cloud marketplaces and inference providers listed on each model page.

Does OpenAI release open-weight models?

Yes: gpt-oss-120b and gpt-oss-20b under Apache 2.0. All GPT-6 and GPT-5.x models are proprietary.
