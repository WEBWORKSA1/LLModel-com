The LLM Brief

One short email a week for people who build with language models: what launched, what changed in price, one tool worth trying and one idea worth stealing. Five minutes to read, free, and easy to leave.

What's in each issue

  • Releases that matter. New models and versions, with what changed in plain English and a link to the full spec page.
  • Price watch. Price cuts, new tiers and long-prompt surcharges, with what they mean for a real workload.
  • One tool. A library, service or technique worth ten minutes of your time.
  • One idea. A practical pattern from someone shipping with LLMs.
  • Contest news. New briefs, deadlines and winners.

Why it exists

The LLM field changes every week, and most coverage is either hype or a firehose. The Brief does the filtering: it tells you what changed, whether it matters for what you're building, and where to read more. It's written by the team that keeps the model directory current.

Frequently asked questions

How often will I hear from you?

Once a week, plus an occasional note when something big happens, like a major price change. You can unsubscribe from any email in one click.

Do you sell or share my email address?

No. We use it to send the newsletter and nothing else. Sponsored placements in the newsletter are labeled, and sponsors never receive subscriber data.

Can I sponsor the newsletter?

Yes. See the advertise page for newsletter placements and current rates.
