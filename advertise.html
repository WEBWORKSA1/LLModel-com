How sponsorship works here

  • Every sponsored placement is labeled as sponsored.
  • Sponsors never change a ranking, a score, a shortlist order or a verdict.
  • Claims in sponsored copy must be ones we can check.
  • Links in sponsored placements use rel="sponsored".
  • We don't accept ads for products we'd warn readers about.

Programmatic display ads are served by Google AdSense under Google's policies and are separate from direct sponsorships.

Frequently asked questions

Who reads LLModel?

People actively evaluating large language models and the tools around them: engineers comparing APIs and open models, founders estimating costs, product managers choosing vendors and IT teams checking security and licensing. Current traffic and audience figures are in the media kit, which we send on request.

Can sponsors influence reviews or rankings?

No. Rankings come from a published formula and verified data, and editorial text is written independently. Sponsored placements are labeled, live in fixed slots and use rel=sponsored links.

Do you run programmatic display ads?

Yes, through Google AdSense. Those are sold by Google, not by us. Direct sponsorships on this page are separate and booked with us.
