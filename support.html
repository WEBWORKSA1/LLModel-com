What support pays for

  • Data checks. Re-verifying prices, context windows and licenses against provider documentation, and API credits to test models ourselves.
  • New tools. More calculators, deeper comparisons and features readers ask for.
  • People. Paying writers, researchers, developers and video producers to cover more models in more depth.
  • Contests. Prize pools that reward practical, efficient work with LLMs.
  • Reach. Getting the guides and tools in front of the people who need them.

Frequently asked questions

Is my contribution tax-deductible?

No. LLModel.com is an independent publication, not a registered charity, so contributions aren't tax-deductible.

Does supporting LLModel buy any influence?

No. Supporters, sponsors and advertisers have no say over rankings, comparisons or editorial text. That independence is the point of reader support.

How do I pay after pledging?

We email you a secure payment link for the amount and frequency you chose, usually within one business day. Nothing is charged until you complete that link, and you can cancel a monthly contribution anytime.

Can my company support LLModel?

Yes. Companies can sponsor a contest prize pool or a tool, which is labeled as sponsorship. See the advertise page or use the pledge form and mention your company.
