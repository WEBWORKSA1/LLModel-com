Prices and specifications change

Model prices, context windows, output limits, licenses, availability and capabilities change frequently, sometimes without notice. Always confirm details on the provider's own pages before you buy, build or sign anything.

Tool results are estimates

Our calculators and other tools produce estimates from the inputs and assumptions they display. Real costs depend on factors we can't see, such as your exact token counts, caching, discounts, taxes and contracts. Real memory use depends on your runtime and settings. Leave a margin for error.
