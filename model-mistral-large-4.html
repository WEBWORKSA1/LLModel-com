Mistral Large 4

Mistral Large 4 is Mistral's flagship, announced on 6 October 2026 as a public API preview. It is a mixture-of-experts model with about 1.05T total and 52B active parameters, a 1.6B vision encoder and a 1M-token context, combining instruct and reasoning modes. Mistral says the weights will be released openly later in October 2026.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Mistral Large 4 cost?

Mistral Large 4 lists at $1.36 per million input tokens and $4.18 per million output tokens, with cached input at $0.14 (checked October 10, 2026). Mistral list price per Mistral docs. During the public preview the docs show a sale price of $0.68 input / $0.07 cached / $2.09 output. API-only for now: Mistral promises open weights 'by the end of the month' (October 2026), but none are on Hugging Face yet and no license is named, so weights is 'closed' and license is null. Context 1M per Mistral docs. Confirm current rates on the provider's pricing page before you commit.

What is the context window of Mistral Large 4?

Mistral Large 4 accepts up to 1,000,000 tokens of context (about 750,000 words, or 1,500 pages). Long prompts cost more and can be slower, so most apps send only what the task needs.

Is Mistral Large 4 open source?

No. Mistral Large 4 is a proprietary model from Mistral AI; its weights aren't published. You can use it through the Mistral AI API and through the provider's apps.

What is Mistral Large 4 best for?

Mistral AI positions Mistral Large 4 for agents, reasoning, long documents, multimodal. It accepts text, images as input, has a reasoning (thinking) mode and supports tool calling. Test it on a sample of your own tasks before you choose it for production.
