Frequently asked questions

Is a free listing guaranteed?

No. Editorial listings are included when a product is useful to people building with LLMs, is generally available and has public documentation and pricing. We review every submission and reply either way.

Do paid placements affect rankings?

Never. Featured listings and sponsorships appear in labeled slots. They don't change the order of any ranking, shortlist or comparison, and sponsors can't edit our text.

How do I pay?

After we confirm the placement and dates, we send a secure payment link or invoice. Nothing is charged before you approve the details.
