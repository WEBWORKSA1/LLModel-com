Frequently asked questions

Is this really free?

Yes. The recommendation costs you nothing. If you choose to be introduced to an implementation partner and later hire them, the partner may pay LLModel a referral fee. That never affects which models we recommend, and you're never obliged to hire anyone.

Who will contact me?

The LLModel team replies first, by email, within 2 business days. Your details go to implementation partners only if you tick the box asking for introductions, and then only to up to three partners matched to your project.

What kinds of projects can you help with?

Choosing a model, building chatbots and assistants, retrieval-augmented generation over company documents, agents and workflow automation, fine-tuning or self-hosting open models, cutting API costs, and security or compliance reviews of LLM features.

What if my project is small?

Send it anyway. Small teams often get the most from a second opinion before they commit to a model or a vendor. If a partner introduction doesn't make sense at your size, we'll point you to the tools and guides that cover your case.

How do you use my information?

Only to respond to your request and, if you ask, to make introductions. We don't sell personal data. Details are in our privacy policy.
