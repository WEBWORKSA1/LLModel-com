Frequently asked questions

How many LLMs are there?

Thousands of language models exist if you count every fine-tune on Hugging Face. This directory tracks the models most teams actually evaluate: current and recent releases from the major API providers and the leading open-weight labs, with prices and specs checked against each provider's documentation.

What does blended price mean?

Blended price combines input and output rates into one number using a three-to-one ratio of input to output tokens, which is typical of chat and retrieval apps. It's a quick way to compare models; use the cost calculator for your real token mix.

What do the tiers mean?

Tiers group models by how their providers position them and how they place on public benchmarks: frontier for the most capable models, advanced for strong mid-priced models, efficient for fast, low-cost models and compact for small models that run on modest hardware. Tiers are our editorial grouping, not our own test results.

Why do some models show no price?

Some open-weight models aren't sold per token by a major host, and some new or preview models don't have a confirmed list price yet. We only show prices we could confirm on an official pricing page.
