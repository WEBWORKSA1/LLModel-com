Granite 4.2 8B

Granite 4.2 8B is the mid-size model of IBM's Granite 4.2 family, released in August 2026 under Apache 2.0. It shares the 30B model's thinking, non-thinking and low-effort modes and OpenAI-style function calling. At about 9B parameters it fits a single 24 GB GPU at BF16.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Granite 4.2 8B cost?

Granite 4.2 8B lists at $0.06 per million input tokens and $0.25 per million output tokens, with cached input at $0.015 (checked October 10, 2026). DeepInfra serverless price (131,072 context). Native context is 128K; IBM says it can be extended to 512K. Confirm current rates on the provider's pricing page before you commit.

What is the context window of Granite 4.2 8B?

Granite 4.2 8B accepts up to 131,072 tokens of context (about 98,000 words, or 200 pages). Long prompts cost more and can be slower, so most apps send only what the task needs.

Is Granite 4.2 8B open source?

Granite 4.2 8B is an open-weight model released under the Apache-2.0 license. You can download the weights from Hugging Face (ibm-granite/granite-4.2-8b) and run or fine-tune them yourself, within the license terms. Open weights isn't the same as fully open source: training data and code may not be published.

Can I run Granite 4.2 8B locally?

Yes, if you have enough memory. The weights alone need about 5 GB at 4-bit quantization and about 18 GB at 16-bit, plus room for the KV cache. Use the VRAM calculator on LLModel.com to check a specific GPU or Mac.

What is Granite 4.2 8B best for?

IBM positions Granite 4.2 8B for enterprise, self-hosting, low latency, fine-tuning. It accepts text as input, has a reasoning (thinking) mode and supports tool calling. Test it on a sample of your own tasks before you choose it for production.
