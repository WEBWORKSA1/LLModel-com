Last updated October 10, 2026

About the name LLModel

"LLModel" is used on this site as a descriptive domain name for a website about large language models: "LLM", the common abbreviation for large language model, combined with "model". The terms "LLM", "large language model" and "language model" are generic technical terms. We claim no exclusive rights in those generic terms and don't use the name to suggest any connection with another company, product or project.

LLModel.com is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any business, product or open-source project that uses a similar name. In particular, it isn't affiliated with Nomic AI or the GPT4All project, whose open-source code has used "llmodel" as the name of an internal software library. Other parties may own trademark registrations that include "LLM" for particular goods or services; our use of the term is limited to its descriptive, technical meaning. "LLM" is also an abbreviation for the Master of Laws degree, which this site has nothing to do with.
