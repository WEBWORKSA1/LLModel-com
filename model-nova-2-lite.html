Amazon Nova 2 Lite

Amazon Nova 2 Lite is a reasoning model from Amazon for everyday workloads, available through Amazon Bedrock since December 2, 2025. It accepts text, images, video and documents in a 1M-token context, and its extended thinking is off by default with three selectable levels.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Amazon Nova 2 Lite cost?

We couldn't confirm a published per-token list price for Amazon Nova 2 Lite on October 10, 2026. Prices not verified: the Nova rate tables on AWS's Bedrock pricing page did not load during verification. OpenRouter lists $0.30 input and $2.50 output per 1M tokens for this model via Amazon Bedrock. Check the provider's pricing page for current rates.

What is the context window of Amazon Nova 2 Lite?

Amazon Nova 2 Lite accepts up to 1,000,000 tokens of context (about 750,000 words, or 1,500 pages), and it can write up to 64K tokens in a single response. Long prompts cost more and can be slower, so most apps send only what the task needs.

Is Amazon Nova 2 Lite open source?

No. Amazon Nova 2 Lite is a proprietary model from Amazon (AWS); its weights aren't published. You can use it through the Amazon (AWS) API and through Amazon Bedrock, OpenRouter.

What is Amazon Nova 2 Lite best for?

Amazon (AWS) positions Amazon Nova 2 Lite for high volume, multimodal, agents, enterprise. It accepts text, images, video, pdf as input, has a reasoning (thinking) mode and supports tool calling. Test it on a sample of your own tasks before you choose it for production.
